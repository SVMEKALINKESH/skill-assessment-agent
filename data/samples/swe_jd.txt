Senior Backend Engineer — FinCo

We're building the next generation of payments infrastructure and looking for a
Senior Backend Engineer to join our core platform team.

About the role
You'll own services that handle millions of transactions per day. You'll work
closely with Product, SRE, and Risk teams to design, build, and operate reliable
distributed systems.

Required skills
- 5+ years of backend experience in Python or Go
- Strong experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Deep understanding of REST API design and versioning
- 3+ years of experience with AWS (Lambda, ECS, RDS, SQS)
- Solid grasp of Docker and container-based deployments
- Experience writing unit and integration tests (expect TDD-friendly workflows)
- Familiarity with observability tools (Datadog, Prometheus, or similar)

Nice to have
- Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, Kinesis)
- Kubernetes production experience
- gRPC and protobuf familiarity
- Experience with financial or payments systems

Soft skills
- Strong written communication (design docs, RFCs)
- Comfortable mentoring junior engineers
- Able to lead scoping discussions with product and cross-functional partners
